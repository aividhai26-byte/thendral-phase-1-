Technical Specifications

Structure

RCC framed structure designed for seismic compliance (Zone III). 200mm for external walls and 100mm for internal walls. Floor-to-floor height maintained at 2950mm.

Flooring

Living & bedrooms feature 600×600mm premium anti-skid vitrified tiles. Common areas utilize premium Italian marble or I-marble equivalents.

Doors & Windows

Main door finished with veneer and fitted with a digital lock. All window openings feature granite stone frames with high-quality aluminium anodized / UPVC sections and glass.

Kitchen

Granite kitchen platform with sink of FRANKE or equivalent make. Piped gas connection on platform; provision for RO water purifier; vitrified tile dado up to lintel level.

Bathrooms

Equipped with KOHLER or JAQUAR CP fittings and sanitary ware. Granite flooring with vitrified tile dado. Provision for instant geysers included.

Lifts & Security

Two fully automatic passenger elevators (SCHINDLER / KONE) per building, plus one service elevator. CCTV coverage in campus, reception, and basement with video door phones.
